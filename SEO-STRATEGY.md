## Internal linking rule

Every supporting article should link contextually to at least two commercial or authority pages, with `/weddings-in-udaipur/` receiving the strongest relevant links. Avoid repeating the exact same anchor on every article.

Preferred journey:

`Journal article → Udaipur guide → relevant service page → real wedding → Contact`

## Real-wedding authority

Every portfolio story should use a descriptive title and location context where accurate, for example:

`Couple Name — Destination Wedding in Udaipur`

Each case study should document the venue, guest count, function mix, design approach, hospitality, and the planning problem solved. Use original wedding photography wherever possible.

## Local SEO actions outside the repository

- Keep the Google Business Profile complete and consistent with the website identity.
- Request genuine reviews from real clients and encourage specific, truthful mentions of the service and location.
- Maintain consistent Nikhil Salvi / Wedding Vows by Nikhil brand details across Instagram, YouTube, WedMeGood and other legitimate profiles.
- Pursue editorial and partner mentions from relevant wedding, venue, Rajasthan travel and event publications.
- Prefer relevant, earned links over bulk link packages.
