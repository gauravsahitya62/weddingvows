How We Do It?

Our Approach

Step

ONE

Tell us your story.

Whether you’re planning a luxury, destination or a low-key wedding, we want to learn more about your story, vision, goals, budget, inspiration and challenges surrounding your upcoming celebration. Together, we can dream up an initial design and ideas to begin crafting your perfect day.

Step

TWO

We’ll establish a firm plan!

Based on your vision and our initial meeting, We will begin to build your event from the ground up. We secure & suggest vendors, curate the event, design all your details and present the best options for you to make the perfect decisions for you and your event.

Step

THREE

It’s all in the details

As an experienced event team, we guide you and assist in establishing your event’s logistics, timelines and checklists. This step is the most important as it allows your day and event to run seamlessly with all your beautiful details, goals and vision incorporated.

Step

FOUR

Let’s get you married!

The plan is in place. We’ve ironed out all the details. Now, it’s time for us to do the work and for you to enjoy all of the wonderful decisions you made.

At The Shaadi Knot, we handle everything from event production to coordinating the plan with your vendors and all that falls in between.
