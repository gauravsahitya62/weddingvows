FAQs

Questions, answered in advance

Let your wedding planner answer the things couples ask us most — before you even have to ask.

How much time is needed to plan a wedding?

Ideally, book your venue 8–9 months in advance. Artists usually need around 3 months and décor planning 1–2 months — we will handle the rest, step by step.

Why should I choose you as my wedding planner?

Wedding Vows by Nikhil is an Udaipur-based team that takes complete responsibility for planning, coordination, and on-ground execution — so you and your family can enjoy every moment.

What should I ask a planner before a destination wedding in Udaipur?

Ask about venue relationships, guest hospitality, artist timelines, décor lead times, and who will be on-site on the wedding days. We walk through all of this in the first consultation.

Can you plan my wedding if my venue is already booked?

Yes. We regularly step in after a venue is confirmed and take over design, vendors, hospitality, and day-of execution.

Do I need a planner for a small or intimate wedding?

Even intimate celebrations benefit from one dedicated team. We scale the plan to your guest count without losing the details that make it feel personal.

How early should I contact you for a wedding in Udaipur?

As soon as you have a season in mind. Peak palace dates in Udaipur fill early — eight to twelve months is ideal.

Do you work with the couple’s chosen vendors?

Yes. We happily collaborate with your preferred artists and vendors, and we also bring our trusted roster when you want us to curate the full team.

Do you help with wedding timelines and schedules?

Yes. We build minute-by-minute run of shows for every function, brief every vendor, and stay on the floor until the last farewell.
